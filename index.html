Common questions

Quick answers to the most frequently asked questions.

How do I verify my age for a Hack Club event?

Go to Hack Club Auth and upload a photo ID (passport, driver's license, or school ID). Verification usually takes less than a day. Make sure your name and date of birth are clearly visible.

How do I apply to start a Hack Club?

Any high school student can start a Hack Club. Fill out the application at apply.hackclub.com — it takes about 10 minutes. Once approved, you'll get access to workshops, a Slack community, and more.

How do I set up Hackatime to track my coding hours?

Visit hackatime.hackclub.com/my/wakatime_setup and follow the setup guide for your editor. Hackatime works with VS Code, Neovim, JetBrains IDEs, and any editor that supports WakaTime. If your editor isn't supported, use Lapse to record a time-lapse instead.

How do I get support for my HCB organization?

For general questions, post in #hcb on Slack. For urgent issues (lost card, frozen funds), email hcb@hackclub.com or call 1-844-237-2290.

I can't find the help channel for my event. What do I do?

Drop a message in #help on Slack and someone will point you to the right place. You can also search the event channels below — if yours isn't listed, #help is the right place to start.

What is the Hack Club phone number?

You can reach Hack Club toll-free at 1-855-625-HACK (4225). For urgent HCB banking issues specifically, call 1-844-237-2290.
